Privacy Policy

WL Reels, by WL Automations · Last updated 17 October 2026

What we collect

When WL Automations connects its own TikTok account to WL Reels, TikTok gives us an access token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar). We use these only to publish our own videos to that account and to check that a post went through.

What we don't collect

WL Reels does not collect information about other TikTok users or viewers, does not read anyone's messages, and does not use cookies or tracking on this site.

Where it's stored

Access tokens are stored privately in WL Automations' automation account (Make.com) and are never published. Videos are hosted on this site so TikTok can fetch them.

How long we keep it

Tokens are kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting WL Reels in TikTok's settings revokes access, and we delete the stored tokens.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this information with anyone.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it.
